Questions? We've got answers.
How is the acceptance probability calculated?▾

We use a proprietary model trained on historical admissions data, factoring in your GPA, test scores, extracurriculars, essay quality, demographics, financial fit, and school-specific admit rates. It's not a guarantee — it's the most accurate estimate available without being inside the admissions office.

Can I upgrade my plan later?▾

Absolutely. You can start with the Snapshot Report and upgrade to Pro or Admission Pass at any time. We'll credit your Snapshot purchase toward the upgrade.

Is my data private and secure?▾

100%. Your data is encrypted and never shared with universities, counselors, or third parties. We use it solely to generate your personalized analysis.

What if I haven't taken the SAT/ACT yet?▾

No problem. We'll calculate your probability based on everything else and show you how different test scores would change your chances. You can add scores later and your report updates instantly.

How is this different from a college counselor?▾

College counselors charge $150–$400/hr and give subjective advice. AdmitMap is data-driven, available instantly, and costs a fraction of a single counseling session. Think of it as having a 24/7 admissions analyst that never sleeps.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription?▾

Yes, anytime with one click. No hidden fees, no cancellation hoops. You keep access through the end of your billing period.

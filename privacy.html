What this policy covers

This policy explains what information AdmitMap collects when you use our website, funnel, account and reports (the "Service"), why we collect it, who else touches it, and what control you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses AdmitMap, wherever you are.

Using the Service is also governed by our Terms of Service.

02

Who is responsible for your data

AdmitMap is operated by Arlan Nurmanov, an individual sole operator based in the Republic of Kazakhstan. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the "data controller" — meaning we decide what is collected and why, and we are the ones you hold accountable.

Contact for any privacy matter, including deletion requests: support@admitmap.com.

05

Essays and AI processing

Your essay is the most personal thing you can give us, so we handle it differently from everything else.

  • Essay text is sent to us only when you ask for it to be graded.
  • Our server forwards it to Anthropic's API, which scores it against our rubric and returns the result.
  • Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models.
  • We do not retain your essay text on our servers after grading. It is processed in the request and discarded. We keep only the resulting numeric scores and written feedback so your report stays intact.
  • No human at AdmitMap reads your essay as a matter of course. We do not review essays to improve our rubric.

Because we do not store the essay, we also cannot recover it for you. Keep your own copy.

Automated decision-making

Your estimates and scores are produced automatically by software. They have no legal effect on you and do not determine anything — no college sees them and no admission outcome depends on them. If you want a human to look at how a number was produced, email us and we will explain it.

06

Data stored in your own browser

As you move through the funnel, your answers are saved in your browser's local storage — a storage area on your own device, under keys such as admitmap_profile. This is what stops you losing your work if you refresh or close the tab.

Data held there stays on that device, in that browser. Clearing your browser data deletes it permanently, including for us — we cannot recover it. Once you create an account, your profile is also saved on our servers so you can return to it from another device.

08

Email and marketing

If you give us your email address — whether you complete a purchase or only go through the funnel — we may send you:

  • Transactional email: receipts, licence keys, password resets, and messages about something you bought. You cannot unsubscribe from these while you have an account, because they are how the Service works.
  • Marketing email: application deadline reminders, product news, and offers, including discounts on further reports.

Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and we honour it promptly. You can also unsubscribe any time by emailing support@admitmap.com. Unsubscribing from marketing does not affect access to anything you have purchased.

If you are in the EU, the UK, or another region with opt-in email rules, you may withdraw consent or object to marketing at any time using the same links, and we will stop.

We do not sell or rent your email address, and we never give it to another company to use for that company's own marketing.

Advertising audiences

We advertise AdmitMap on platforms such as Meta (Instagram and Facebook), TikTok and Google. To do that, we may upload a list of email addresses to those platforms so they can:

  • match existing users, so we can show you offers or, equally, stop showing you ads for something you already bought; and
  • build a "lookalike" audience — the platform finds other people with broadly similar characteristics, and shows our ads to them instead.

What this means in practice:

  • Email addresses are sent hashed — scrambled into an irreversible code — so the platform can match them without reading them.
  • The platform is contractually permitted to use the list only to build our audience, and for nothing else of its own.
  • We never upload your grades, test scores, income band, school list, essays, or report contents to an advertising platform. Only email addresses.
  • We do not learn who ends up in a lookalike audience, and those people are strangers to us unless they click an ad and sign up themselves.

To opt out, email support@admitmap.com and ask us to exclude you from advertising audiences, and we will remove you from any list we upload. Unsubscribing from marketing email also removes you. You can additionally limit ad targeting inside each platform's own ad settings.

09

Cookies and similar technology

We keep this deliberately minimal:

  • Strictly necessary — a session cookie to keep you signed in, and local storage to hold your funnel progress. These cannot be turned off without breaking the Service.
  • Payment — Lemon Squeezy may set its own cookies during checkout, governed by their policy.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, session-replay recording, or third-party behavioural advertising networks on this site. We advertise AdmitMap elsewhere, as described in section 8, but that is done from an email list rather than by tracking you around the web.

If we ever add analytics or an advertising pixel to the site, we will update this section before it goes live, name the provider, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no common industry standard for how to respond to one. We do not track you across other websites at all, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off, and we do not respond to it. If that ever changes, we will say so here.

10

Aggregated and de-identified data

We may combine information across many users to produce aggregated or de-identified statistics — for example, the average profile of students applying to a given school, or how accurate our estimates turn out to be across thousands of applications. Before this is done, anything that identifies you is removed, and we do not attempt to re-identify it afterwards.

Aggregated and de-identified data is not personal information, and we may use and publish it to improve our models, check our data quality, and write about admissions trends. Nothing in it names you, quotes you, or can be traced back to you.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), password hashing rather than plaintext storage, restricted access to production systems, API keys held only on the server and never exposed to the browser, and origin restrictions on our endpoints.

No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data and creates a real risk to you, we will notify you and any regulator we are required to notify, without undue delay.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct — fix anything inaccurate;
  • Delete — erase your data and close your account;
  • Export — receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • Object or restrict — object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to pause processing;
  • Withdraw consent — where processing relies on consent, withdraw it at any time, without affecting what happened before;
  • Opt out of marketing — at any time, via unsubscribe or email;
  • Opt out of advertising audiences — ask us to exclude your email address from any list we upload to an advertising platform.

Email support@admitmap.com. We respond within 30 days and never charge for it. We may need to confirm your identity before acting, to make sure we are not handing your data to someone else.

If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland

The rights above are your GDPR / UK GDPR rights. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We would rather you came to us first, but that right stands either way.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request a copy, to request deletion, to correct inaccuracies, and to not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA. We do provide hashed email addresses to advertising platforms to build audiences, as described in section 8; some regulators treat that as "sharing", so to be safe we treat it as opt-out-able — email us and we will exclude you. Use the same email address to make any request.

Verification. Before we act on a request to access, correct, or delete data, we have to be reasonably sure the request is really from you — otherwise we would be handing your academic profile to a stranger. We will normally verify by asking you to send the request from the email address on the account. If we cannot verify you, we will tell you why rather than simply refusing.

Authorized agents. You may appoint someone to make a request on your behalf. We will ask for written proof that you authorised them, and we may still ask you to confirm your identity directly.

California "Shine the Light". California residents may ask, once a year and free of charge, what personal information a business disclosed to third parties for those third parties' own direct marketing. We do not disclose personal information to anyone for their own direct marketing, so there is nothing to report — but you are welcome to ask and we will confirm that in writing.

How to delete your data

Two ways, and both work:

  • Clear your browser data. This permanently removes everything held locally on that device.
  • Email support@admitmap.com asking us to delete your account. We erase your account, academic profile, school list, scores and reports from our systems, and confirm when it is done.

The only things that survive a deletion request are purchase records we are legally obliged to keep for tax purposes, and the minimal suppression record that stops us emailing you again. Deleted reports cannot be restored.

Children

AdmitMap is available to people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must agree to our Terms of Service, must be the one who makes any purchase, and is responsible for the information entered. We treat that parent or guardian as the account holder.

Parents and guardians can ask us at any time to show them what we hold about their child, to correct it, or to delete it and close the account. Email support@admitmap.com from any address and we will verify and act on it.

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If we discover that we have, we delete it immediately. If you are a parent who believes a child under 13 has given us information, email support@admitmap.com and we will remove it right away.

    What separates an admitted essay
    01
    Open inside a moment
    Drop the reader into a specific scene — a place, an hour, a problem. Never open with a thesis about yourself.
    02
    One story, not five
    A highlight reel reads like a résumé. Officers remember the student who went deep on a single thing that mattered.
    03
    Name real details
    Concrete nouns and numbers prove it happened. "Forty-three jars of honey" beats "I learned responsibility."
    04
    End with earned insight
    Show what actually changed in how you think. Reflection you didn't pay for in the story reads hollow.

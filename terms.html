03

What AdmitMap is

You give us information about your academic profile — grades, test scores, coursework, activities, honors, an approximate family income band, the schools on your list, and optionally an essay. We combine that with public data, primarily the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard and institutional Common Data Sets, to produce:

  • an estimated acceptance probability for each school on your list;
  • a breakdown of the factors that drive that estimate;
  • an estimated net price by income band for each school, where such data exists;
  • a comparison of your profile against the profiles of admitted students;
  • deadline information for each application round;
  • and, where you request it, automated feedback on an essay.

AdmitMap does not submit applications, contact colleges on your behalf, guarantee admission, guarantee financial aid, or provide counseling by a human being.

04

Estimates, not promises

This is the most important section of these Terms. Read it carefully.

Every number AdmitMap shows you is a statistical estimate, not a prediction and not a promise. Specifically:

  • Acceptance probabilities are estimates. They are calculated from historical, publicly reported admissions data for classes that have already been admitted. They do not account for factors we cannot see and colleges do not publish — institutional priorities in a given year, the strength of that year's applicant pool, recommendation letters, interviews, demonstrated interest, legacy or donor relationships, athletic recruitment, departmental needs, or the judgment of the individual reading your file.
  • Admissions decisions are made solely by colleges. We have no relationship with, influence over, or advance knowledge of any admissions office. A high estimate is not an offer and does not make admission likely for you personally. A low estimate does not mean you should not apply.
  • Financial aid figures are estimates. Net price figures are modelled from published averages by income band. Your actual aid award depends on a full financial review by each school. Always confirm cost using each school's own Net Price Calculator and financial aid office before making any financial decision.
  • Essay and activity scores are automated opinions. They are produced by software against our own rubric. They are not the judgment of an admissions officer and no college uses or recognises our scores.
  • Data can be wrong or out of date. We source from public datasets that are published on a lag and sometimes contain errors. Where we do not have verified data for a school, we show "—" rather than a guess, but we cannot guarantee that every figure shown is current or accurate.

AdmitMap is information, not professional advice. Nothing in the Service is educational counseling, financial advice, tax advice, or legal advice. Decisions about where to apply, where to enrol, and how to pay for college are yours. You should treat AdmitMap as one input among several, alongside your school counselor, the colleges themselves, and your family.

You agree that you will not rely on AdmitMap as the sole basis for any application, enrolment, or financial decision.

Ever since I was a little kid, soccer has been my passion. I have played on a team since I was five years old, and it has taught me so many valuable life lessons about hard work, teamwork and never giving up.

My sophomore year was the most challenging year of my life. In the second game of the season I tore my ACL and the doctor told me I would be out for the entire year. I was devastated. Soccer was my whole identity and I did not know who I was without it. I felt like my world was falling apart.

But I did not give up. I went to physical therapy three times a week and worked harder than I ever had before. There were many days when I wanted to quit, but I kept pushing through the pain. I also went to every single practice and game to support my teammates from the sidelines, because a true team player is always there for their team.

After eight long months of hard work, I was finally cleared to play again. My first game back was one of the best moments of my life. When I stepped onto the field, I felt like all my hard work had paid off. We ended up winning the conference championship that year and I was named captain the next season.

This experience taught me that with hard work and determination, you can overcome any obstacle life throws at you. It made me a stronger person both physically and mentally. I learned that setbacks are just opportunities for a comeback. I will bring this same work ethic and determination to college, and I know that I will be able to overcome any challenge that comes my way.

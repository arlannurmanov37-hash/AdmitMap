Who these terms are for

These Creator Program Terms ("Creator Terms") apply to you if AdmitMap has invited you to share a personal AdmitMap link and earn a commission on reports bought through it (the "Program"). AdmitMap is operated by Arlan Nurmanov, an individual sole operator based in the Republic of Kazakhstan ("AdmitMap", "we", "us").

You must be at least 18 years old to join the Program. By sharing your link, accepting a commission or using your creator dashboard, you agree to these Creator Terms. Our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply; where they conflict with these Creator Terms on anything about the Program, these Creator Terms win.

Joining the Program does not make you our employee, agent or partner. You are an independent creator, and you may not make promises, sign anything or speak on behalf of AdmitMap.

02

Your link and how sales are credited

You receive one personal link, for example www.admitmap.app/?ref=yourname, and a private dashboard link. Your link never expires. Keep the dashboard link to yourself; anyone who has it can see your dashboard.

  • 30 days from the last visit. When someone opens your link, they are credited to you for 30 days. If they buy a report within those 30 days, the sale is yours. Each time they open your link again, the 30 days start over.
  • Last link wins. If the same person opens another creator's link after yours, the sale is credited to the creator whose link they opened last.
  • What does not count: purchases you make yourself or for your own household; purchases made with a 100% discount code; purchases that are refunded or charged back; and purchases we reasonably believe are fraudulent, self-generated or made in breach of these Creator Terms.
  • Our records decide. Visits and sales are counted by our systems and shown in your dashboard. If a technical problem means a sale was not recorded, tell us within 30 days and we will look into it in good faith, but our records are final.
03

Commission

You earn a fixed commission for every report bought through your link. The rate depends on how many reports have been bought through your link in total (your "lifetime sales"), and a new level applies from the sale that reaches it. Your lifetime sales keep counting — reaching a level does not reset them (after your 100th sale you are at 101 of 250 toward the next level).

04

Payouts

  • 14-day hold. A commission becomes payable 14 days after the purchase. If the purchase is refunded or charged back, no commission is earned on it — this is why we wait 14 days.
  • Monthly. We pay once a month, for commissions that have become payable, usually within the first days of the month.
  • Minimum $25. If your payable balance is under $25, it rolls over to the next month.
  • Method. We pay by PayPal or Wise to the details you give us. You are responsible for giving us correct details; we are not responsible for money sent to details you provided. Transfer fees charged by PayPal or Wise are paid by you and deducted from the payout.
  • Reversals. If a sale is refunded, charged back or found to be invalid after we paid you, we may deduct that commission from your future payouts.
  • Taxes. You are responsible for reporting and paying any taxes on what you earn. If we need tax information from you to pay you lawfully, we may hold payouts until you provide it.
05

Honest promotion and disclosure

You must make it clear that you are paid for promoting AdmitMap. On every post, video or story that includes your link or talks about AdmitMap, use a clear label such as #ad or the platform's "Paid partnership" tag, as required by the US Federal Trade Commission and by the platforms themselves.

When you talk about AdmitMap you must be accurate. In particular, you must not:

  • call the report free — reports are paid ($19 or $29);
  • promise or suggest that AdmitMap guarantees admission, scholarships or financial aid, or that its numbers are anything other than estimates based on public data;
  • invent results, reviews or testimonials, or present someone else's results as your own;
  • imply that AdmitMap is endorsed by, affiliated with or approved by any college, the Common App, the College Board or any government body.

Showing your own real report and your honest opinion of it is exactly what we want.

06

What you must not do

  • Send unsolicited messages (spam) — by email, direct message, comments or any other channel — to promote your link.
  • Contact anyone under 18 directly to promote AdmitMap. Public posts are fine; private outreach to minors is not.
  • Buy ads on search engines or social platforms that use the words "AdmitMap" or "Admit Map", or that impersonate AdmitMap, without our written permission.
  • Use your link on coupon or deal sites, or pay people to click or buy.
  • Use bots, fake accounts, click farms or any trick to inflate visits or sales.
  • Use our name, logo or report designs in a way that could make people think your content comes from AdmitMap itself. You may show your own report and use our logo to refer to us.
07

Privacy

Your dashboard only shows totals: visits, completed forms, purchases and your earnings. It never shows who visited or bought, and you must not try to find out. We keep your name, contact and payout details to run the Program and pay you, as described in our Privacy Policy.

08

Pausing and ending

You can leave the Program at any time by telling us. We can pause your link or end your participation at any time, for example if you breach these Creator Terms. If we end it for a breach, commissions connected to the breach are forfeited. Otherwise, commissions earned on valid sales before the end are paid as usual once they become payable and reach the minimum, or in a final payout within 60 days if they do not.

We may change or close the Program. We will tell you at least 14 days in advance, except where a change is needed for legal reasons or to stop abuse.

You are responsible for your own content and agree to cover us for claims that arise from it, including claims that it breached a law, a platform's rules or someone else's rights.
